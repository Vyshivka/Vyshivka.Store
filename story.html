● Roots & Heritage

The Heart Behind Every Stitch

Vyshivka was born out of a deep love for traditional Indian hand-embroidery and the belief that clothing should carry a soul. Rooted in Jaggampeta, AP, our home studio is a quiet corner where needles, silk threads, and organic fabrics meet contemporary imagination.

In a world of mass-produced fast fashion, we wanted to build something intimate. Every single petal, leaf, and monogram is meticulously hand-stitched with patience and precision. Whether you bring your own favorite garment or let us source a pure silk piece for you, our mission is to weave your personal story directly into the fabric.

"Handcrafted elegance isn't just about how a garment looks—it’s about the warmth and care stitched into every single thread."

From everyday silk shirts to festive lehengas and delicate children's wear, we take pride in personal creations that stay with you through life's most meaningful moments. Thank you for letting us be a part of your journey.
