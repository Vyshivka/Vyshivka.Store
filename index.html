About Our Atelier

Rooted in Heritage

Based in Jaggampeta, Andhra Pradesh, Vyshivka Studio brings traditional Indian hand-embroidery and fine needlework to your everyday wardrobe. Every garment is meticulously hand-stitched by master artisans, blending intricate threadwork, cultural legacy, and bespoke elegance tailored specifically to your vision.
